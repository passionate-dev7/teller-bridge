The Story of Teller

Moving a stablecoin between Bitcoin Cash and Base meant a bridge, a custodian or a size limit. Teller is a desk with owned inventory. The card shows what the desk can fill right now, per direction, read from the desk wallets. A swap up to that number is atomic: the user coins sit in a hash-timelock on one chain, the desk coins sit in a hash-timelock on the other, and one 32-byte secret opens both. Above it, a live NEAR Intents quote, so no size fails.

Benefits of Teller

  1. 0.50 PUSD to USDC takes one WizardConnect signature, no Base wallet and no Base gas
  2. USDC to PUSD needs no BCH signature: the desk relays the claim and it settles in one block
  3. Live inventory on the card: "fills up to" per direction, read from the desk wallets
  4. Non-custody by construction: if the desk stalls, both locks refund to the user
  5. 70 node tests and 16 forge tests pass, 14 covenant and 18 Base contract mutants killed
  6. Past swaps replay from the desk record, every leg linked to its explorer
